My Journey

From curious beginner to experienced professional, here's how my passion for technology and design evolved into a fulfilling career.

My fascination with technology began during my computer science studies, where I discovered the perfect intersection of logic and creativity. What started as curiosity about how websites work quickly evolved into a deep passion for creating digital experiences that not only function flawlessly but also delight users.

Over the past three years, I've had the privilege of working on diverse projects ranging from enterprise web applications to innovative mobile solutions. Each project has been a learning opportunity, pushing me to explore new technologies and refine my approach to problem-solving.
